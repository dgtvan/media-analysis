Lead with the most striking shot. Follow the order things happened unless a different order tells the story better.
Prefer sharp, steady, well-lit shots; skip shaky or blurry parts. Vary wide and close shots, keep each shot short,
and let the best moment run a little longer. Keep short spoken moments that add feeling or context.
