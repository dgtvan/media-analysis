---
id: meeting
name: Meeting
description: A work meeting where several people discuss an agenda, make decisions and agree on follow-ups.
signals:
  - several participants taking turns on shared topics
  - talk of decisions, owners, deadlines, next steps, status updates
  - agenda items, project or team names
evidence:
  - slides, documents, dashboards or boards shared on screen while being discussed
  - task lists, timelines or numbers shown on screen
sections:
  - Discussion
---
Write for someone who missed the meeting and needs to act on it. The Conclusion gives the decisions and who does
what; the details let them check where each one came from.

- Discussion: one short paragraph or a few bullets per topic, in the order discussed: the key points, who raised
  them, questions asked and their answers, and how the topic ended. Use names or roles as they appear in the
  conversation; if a name is unknown, say so instead of inventing one.
