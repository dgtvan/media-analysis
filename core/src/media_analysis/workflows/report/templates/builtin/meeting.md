---
id: meeting
name: Meeting
description: A work meeting where several people discuss an agenda, make decisions and agree on follow-ups.
signals:
  - several participants taking turns on shared topics
  - talk of decisions, owners, deadlines, next steps, status updates
  - agenda items, project or team names
evidence:
  - slides, documents, dashboards or boards shared on screen while being discussed
  - task lists, timelines or numbers shown on screen
sections:
  - Summary
  - Participants
  - Agenda and discussion
  - Questions raised
  - Decisions and conclusions
  - Action items
  - Open issues
---
Write for someone who missed the meeting and needs to act on it.

- Summary: 3-6 sentences covering purpose, main outcomes and anything urgent.
- Participants: names or roles as they appear in the conversation; if names are unknown, say so instead of inventing them.
- Agenda and discussion: one subsection per topic, in the order discussed, with the key points and who raised them.
- Questions raised: each question, who asked it, and the answer if one was given (mark unanswered ones).
- Decisions and conclusions: only things that were actually agreed.
- Action items: a table with columns Task | Assignee | Due | Source (timecode). Use "unassigned" / "not stated" when missing.
- Open issues: unresolved points, risks, disagreements.
