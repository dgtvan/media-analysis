---
id: interview
name: Interview
description: A question-and-answer conversation where one side interviews the other (job interview, podcast interview, user research, Q&A).
signals:
  - one person mostly asks questions, the other mostly answers
  - introductions, background questions, follow-up probing
evidence:
  - documents, portfolios, designs or prototypes shown during the conversation
sections:
  - Summary
  - Participants
  - Questions and answers
  - Key insights
  - Notable quotes
  - Follow-ups
---
Write for someone who needs the substance of the interview quickly.

- Participants: interviewer and interviewee, by name or role.
- Questions and answers: each question (paraphrased if long) followed by a faithful summary of the answer, with timecode.
- Key insights: the most important things learned, as bullet points.
- Notable quotes: short verbatim quotes with timecodes (translate to English if needed, keeping the original in brackets).
- Follow-ups: next steps, promised materials, open points.
