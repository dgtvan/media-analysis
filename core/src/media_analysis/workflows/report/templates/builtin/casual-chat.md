---
id: casual-chat
name: Casual chat
description: An informal conversation between friends, family or colleagues without a work agenda.
signals:
  - relaxed, informal tone; jokes, personal stories
  - topics change freely; no agenda, decisions or tasks
evidence:
  - photos, videos or websites shared during the chat
sections:
  - Summary
  - Participants
  - Topics discussed
  - Plans and commitments
  - Notable moments
---
Keep it short and friendly.

- Topics discussed: one bullet per topic with the gist and who brought it up.
- Plans and commitments: anything someone agreed to do (meet up, send something), with timecode. Omit if none.
- Notable moments: funny or important moments, with timecodes.
