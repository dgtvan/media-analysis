---
id: lecture-tutorial
name: Lecture or tutorial
description: One main speaker teaching or demonstrating a topic (lecture, course video, tutorial, talk, demo).
signals:
  - one dominant speaker explaining concepts step by step
  - slides, a whiteboard or a live demonstration
  - phrases like "today we will learn", "in this video", "next step"
evidence:
  - slides with key points, definitions, formulas or diagrams
  - demonstration steps shown on screen (commands, settings, results)
sections:
  - Summary
  - Learning objectives
  - Key concepts
  - Step-by-step walkthrough
  - Examples
  - Key takeaways
  - Further study
---
Write study notes a learner could revise from without rewatching.

- Learning objectives: what the viewer should be able to do or understand afterwards.
- Key concepts: one subsection per concept with a clear explanation in your own words, plus the slide frame if one exists.
- Step-by-step walkthrough: numbered steps for any procedure or demo, with the exact commands/settings shown.
- Examples: worked examples given by the speaker.
- Further study: resources, references or topics the speaker mentioned. Omit if none.
